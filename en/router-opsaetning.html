Router setup

  • Setting up the internet — Kollegienet PDF
  • Opsætning af internet — Kollegienet (Danish) PDF

The following assumes the router uses its factory settings.

Routers

Apple (AirPort)

  1. Open AirPort Utility
  2. Find the “Network” tab
  3. Change “Router Mode” from “Off (Bridge Mode)” to “DHCP and NAT”
  4. Click “Update”

The router will then complain about double NAT — this can be ignored.

Why is this necessary?

Apple routers configure themselves based on the IP address they receive from the network. When they receive an IP address in the private RFC 1918 range, they set themselves to “Off (Bridge Mode)”.

NETGEAR N300 (WNR2000)

We strongly advise against buying this router — we see repeated problems with it, and as it's one of the cheapest on the market, it's unfortunately also the one most students end up buying.

Mercusys AC1200

Must not be used at HCØ and Rasmus Rask Kollegiet.
