About Kollegienet

Foreningen Kollegienet Odense (FKO) is a non-profit association that provides internet to dorms and youth housing in Odense.

The association is run by a board and a day-to-day manager, supported by working groups of volunteers from the connected dorms. The price per user and the association's budget are set every year at the general meeting (repræsentantskabsmøde) — see minutes and accounts under documents.

Working groups

Much of the practical work — operations, support and equipment at the individual dorms — is done by working groups of volunteers from the dorms themselves. If you'd like to join, see the section on volunteering below or contact us.

Chairman

The association's chairman is Anders Siebert Johannessen Jørgensen, who can be contacted at formand@kollegienet.dk. All enquiries to the association should go through the day-to-day manager unless this is not appropriate.

Volunteer

At FKO we're always looking for skilled volunteers who want to spend some of their time on fun social events and working with network equipment. Interested, or just curious to hear more? Contact our day-to-day manager at leder@kollegienet.dk :-)
