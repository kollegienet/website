Become a user

To use Kollegienet, you need to live in one of the dorms or youth housing we provide internet to. If you're not sure whether we provide internet where you live, ask your housing association or contact us. As a user, you automatically have internet through the existing network socket in your home.

Kollegienet is a non-profit association, and the price per user is set at the annual general meeting (repræsentantskabsmøde). The annual accounts and budget are under documents. The price is currently DKK 54.75 per month per user.

The internet agreement is made between Kollegienet and the housing association, so each user/tenant pays the housing association. The fee can either be included in the rent or listed as a separate item — if it's listed separately, it may differ slightly from Kollegienet's fee, depending on the housing association's internal budget.
