Kollegienet in English

Foreningen Kollegienet Odense (FKO) is a non-profit association that provides internet to dorms and youth housing in Odense, run by volunteers. The rest of the website is in Danish.

Network status

See the current status of every dorm on our status page (green: everything OK, red: problems). Facebook is our FAQ and your lifeline: follow Foreningen Kollegienet Odense to stay informed about maintenance work and news.

Router setup

Step-by-step guide: Setting up the internet — Kollegienet (PDF). The following assumes the router uses its factory settings.

Apple (AirPort)

  1. Open AirPort Utility.
  2. Find the “Network” tab.
  3. Change “Router Mode” from “Off (Bridge Mode)” to “DHCP and NAT”.
  4. Click “Update”.

The router will then complain about double NAT — this can be ignored.

Why is this necessary? Apple routers configure themselves based on the IP address they receive from the network. When they receive an address in the private RFC 1918 range, they set themselves to “Off (Bridge Mode)”, which doesn't work well with our network.

NETGEAR N300 (WNR2000)

We strongly advise against buying this router. We see repeated problems with it, and as it is one of the cheapest on the market, it is unfortunately also the one most students end up buying.

Mercusys AC1200

Must not be used at HCØ and Rasmus Rask Kollegiet.
